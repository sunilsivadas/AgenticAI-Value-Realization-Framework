# Change log

Newest first.

## 15 Nov 2026

Full realignment with the article series as published on 15 November 2026 (introduction v17, Part 1 v27, Part 2 v16, Part 3 v7, Part 4 v5, Part 5 v4, Part 6 v8). The previous version dated from 6 June 2026, when only the introduction was out.

**Series table.** All seven articles are now listed as published, with their current titles. Part 6, From framework to ROI, is added.

**Scorecard replaced.** The earlier scorecard asked for a score from 0 to 1 on each factor and multiplied the five. The series does not support a composite score: only cost has a clean unit. The scorecard is now the twelve numbers from Part 6, each with a threshold, an action and an owner.

**New framework files.** `framework/cost-model.md` prices each factor per case and works through the customer-service example. `framework/contract.md` sets out the three-clause contract with questions for a vendor.

**Use-case weights rewritten.** The three archetypes with High, Medium and Low weights are replaced by Part 1's five attributes and its matrix of five use cases, Part 4's action tiers and Part 3's reliability bar per tier.

**Factor pages rewritten.** Each page now summarises its article: the claims, the unit, the levers, the order of operations and the traps.

**Templates rewritten.** The evaluation worksheet follows the decide steps of the build sequence and includes the cost model and kill criteria. The deployment checklist is the ten-step build sequence from Part 6.

**Key figures corrected and updated.**

- The Remote Labor Index was credited to METR. It is from Scale AI and the Center for AI Safety. Its figures are updated: 2.5% at launch, 21.25% in October 2026 and 41.3% in November 2026.
- GDPval was credited to Anthropic and linked to a different paper. It is from OpenAI (arXiv 2510.04374). The top figure is now 84.9% (GPT-5.5, vendor-reported).
- The METR task horizons are corrected to about 12 hours at 50% and about 70 minutes at 80%, for Claude Opus 4.6.
- The Agent Arena "2.3×" figure is removed. The series no longer states it as a number.
- Every figure now carries an evidence label.

**Catalogue replaced.** The earlier list of about 120 third-party tools is removed. The series does not cite them, they had not been checked, and some entries pointed to the wrong repository. The README now lists only benchmarks, tools and standards the series cites. Every link was checked on 14 November 2026.

**Data file rebuilt.** `data/benchmarks.yaml` now holds the series metadata, the units, the key figures with dates and evidence labels, the worked-case inputs and results, and the benchmarks and tools the series cites.

**Contributing guide updated.** New rules for figures, links and style.

**Version numbers.** The introduction (v17) and Part 6 (v8) were republished later the same day with a link to this repository and no other change. The versions above and in `data/benchmarks.yaml` reflect that.
